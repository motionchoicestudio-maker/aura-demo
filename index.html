FAQ

Questions patients ask
before booking

Short answers about visits, pricing, and care. If yours is not here, call us and we will help.

How do I know which service I need?

Start with a consultation: photos, a 3D scan, and an honest conversation. You leave with a clear plan, even if the answer is nothing yet.

Can I get care the same day?

Yes. We hold same-day slots for emergencies every morning, and priority phone support around the clock for urgent cases.

Are prices fixed?

Yes. You get a written quote before treatment starts, and it does not change in the chair. 0% financing is available for larger treatments.

Will treatment hurt?

Modern anesthesia and a gentle pace mean most patients feel pressure, not pain. Anxious patients can ask about sedation options.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your plan details and we will verify coverage before your visit.

Do implants have a warranty?

Implant work comes with a written warranty, and we schedule check-ins during the first year to make sure everything settles well.

The first step to the smile you deserve

Leave a request and our care coordinator will call you back within 15 minutes. Your first consultation with a 3D scan is free.
