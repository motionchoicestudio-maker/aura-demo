Your first consult, previewed.

Three questions patients ask Priya most often, answered the way they are answered in the chair. Periodontics.

My gums bleed when I brush. Serious or normal?

You

Dr. Priya Anand

Common, not normal. Bleeding is the earliest warning, and caught at this stage it’s very fixable.

The dentist said “deep cleaning”. Sounds ominous.

You

Dr. Priya Anand

It’s thorough, not painful. We numb properly, work in quadrants, and your gums feel calmer within days.

Can receding gums grow back?

You

Dr. Priya Anand

Lost tissue doesn’t regrow by itself, but grafting restores it. Gums are the foundation; nobody notices a foundation until it’s ignored.

✦

About

Dr. Anand treats the foundation: gums and bone. She joined AURA from a periodontology fellowship in Zurich and leads our gum-health and grafting practice.

Approach

Her patients keep their teeth longer because problems get caught at the measurement stage, not the pain stage.

Focus areas

Gum disease treatment, soft-tissue grafting, and peri-implant care.
